1. Introduction

Welcome to Muslim Prayer.

Muslim Prayer is an Islamic companion application designed to help users manage their daily prayers and access Islamic resources, including Quran reading, Adhkar, Qibla direction, the Hijri calendar and Ramadan features.

We respect your privacy and aim to collect only the information necessary to operate and improve the application.

This Privacy Policy explains what information may be processed when you use Muslim Prayer, how it is used, and what choices and rights you have.

  • Application: Muslim Prayer
  • Developer / Data Controller: Youssef HASSOUN
  • Contact email: ussef.hassoun@gmail.com
  • Website: https://hassoundev.github.io/muslim-prayer-support/

2. No account required

Muslim Prayer does not require you to create an account or register to use its core features.

We do not ask you to provide your name, phone number or email address to access prayer times, the Quran, Adhkar, Tasbih, Qibla or the Islamic calendar.

3. Location information

Muslim Prayer uses approximate location information to calculate prayer times and determine the Qibla direction.

Location access:

  • Is requested only when you explicitly choose to use the location feature.
  • Uses approximate location rather than precise GPS positioning.
  • Does not require background location access.
  • Is not continuously tracked.
  • Is not intentionally transmitted to Firebase Analytics or advertising services.

Location information may be stored locally on your device to provide prayer times and other location-dependent features.

You can also select a city manually without granting location permission.

You can manage or revoke location permissions through your device settings.

4. Information stored on your device

Muslim Prayer stores certain information locally to provide its features and preserve your preferences.

This may include:

  • Selected city and approximate location coordinates.
  • Prayer calculation method and prayer-time adjustments.
  • Notification preferences and Adhan settings.
  • Quran bookmarks, reading position and progress.
  • Adhkar favorites and reading progress.
  • Tasbih counters and history.
  • Hijri calendar preferences.
  • Ramadan and fasting tracker information.
  • Application settings and language preferences.

This information is primarily stored on your device.

Deleting the application may remove locally stored application information, depending on your device's operating system and backup settings.

5. Prayer notifications

Muslim Prayer can schedule local notifications for prayer times, Adhan and other optional reminders.

These notifications are scheduled using your device's notification services.

The application does not need background location tracking to schedule its regular prayer notifications.

You can enable, disable or customize notifications through the application settings and your device settings.

6. Firebase services

Muslim Prayer uses certain Google Firebase services to support application functionality, diagnostics and improvements.

The Firebase services integrated into the application include:

Firebase Remote Config

Firebase Remote Config allows the application to retrieve configuration settings without requiring a new application release.

According to Google's documentation, Remote Config may automatically process technical information such as:

  • Country code.
  • Device language.
  • Device time zone.
  • Operating system information.
  • Firebase application identifiers.
  • Application bundle information.
  • Firebase installation-related identifiers.

This information may be processed when the application retrieves Remote Config settings.

Remote Config is not used to collect your prayer activity, religious practices, Quran reading history or location coordinates for advertising.

Firebase Analytics

Firebase Analytics may be used to collect application usage and technical information to help understand application performance and improve the user experience.

Analytics collection is subject to the application's privacy settings and consent controls.

Analytics is disabled by default until the applicable consent requirements are satisfied.

Firebase Crashlytics

Firebase Crashlytics may be used to identify application crashes and technical errors.

Crash reporting is subject to the application's privacy settings and consent controls.

You can manage your analytics and crash-reporting preferences through the Privacy section of the application, where available.

Firebase data processing

Firebase services are provided by Google LLC and its affiliates.

Information processed through Firebase may be handled on Google's infrastructure and in accordance with Google's privacy and data protection terms.

For more information:

  • https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy
  • https://policies.google.com/privacy

7. Advertising

The application contains an advertising integration based on Google Mobile Ads and Google's User Messaging Platform.

Advertising is disabled in the initial production release of Muslim Prayer.

No production advertisements are currently intended to be displayed or requested.

If advertising is introduced in a future version, this Privacy Policy will be updated to explain the relevant advertising technologies, consent mechanisms and data processing.

Where required, users will be provided with appropriate advertising privacy choices before advertisements are requested.

Advertising is not intended to use prayer activity, Quran reading history, religious practices or precise location information for targeting.

8. Information sharing

Muslim Prayer does not sell users' personal information.

Information may be processed by third-party service providers where necessary to operate application features, including:

  • Google Firebase services for application configuration, analytics and diagnostics.
  • Google advertising and consent services when applicable in a future advertising-enabled release.

These providers process information according to their respective terms and privacy policies.

We do not intentionally share users' approximate location coordinates or religious activity with advertising providers.

9. Data retention and deletion

Information stored locally remains on your device until it is deleted through application functionality, device settings or application removal, where applicable.

Information processed by third-party providers is subject to their respective retention policies and settings.

You can:

  • Change your privacy and consent preferences through the application settings.
  • Disable location permission through your device settings.
  • Disable notifications through your device settings.
  • Remove locally stored application information by using available application controls or uninstalling the application.

For requests concerning information processed by third-party providers, please contact the relevant provider where appropriate.

10. Your privacy rights

Depending on your country or region, you may have rights concerning your personal information, including:

  • The right to request access to personal information.
  • The right to request correction of inaccurate information.
  • The right to request deletion.
  • The right to object to certain processing.
  • The right to restrict certain processing.
  • The right to withdraw consent where processing is based on consent.
  • The right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

If you are located in the European Economic Area, you may also have rights under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

For privacy-related requests, contact: ussef.hassoun@gmail.com

We will review and respond to requests in accordance with applicable data protection laws.

11. Children's privacy

Muslim Prayer is a general-purpose Islamic application.

The application does not knowingly require children to provide personal information or create accounts.

Parents and guardians should supervise children's use of mobile applications and device permissions.

12. Data security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect information processed through Muslim Prayer.

Information stored locally is subject to the security protections provided by your device and operating system.

Third-party service providers maintain their own security measures and data protection practices.

However, no electronic storage or transmission method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

13. International data transfers

Some third-party service providers used by Muslim Prayer may process information on servers located outside your country of residence.

Where applicable, these providers use their own legal and contractual mechanisms for international data transfers.

Please consult their respective privacy policies for further information.

14. Changes to this Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when application functionality, third-party services or applicable legal requirements change.

The latest version will be made available through the application or the public privacy policy URL.

The date at the top of this document indicates when it was last updated.
